# Optional Coaster Skin chassis appearance

Install the supported **Create: Coaster Skins1.1.0** release (file9409938) with the
project's NeoForge21.1.256, Sable2.0.6 and bundled Veil4.3.2 baseline. The initial
supported graphics profile uses the native renderer without an optional Sodium
or shader loader. No optional appearance mod is required for original vehicles.

1. Stop and capture the train at a valid boarding position, then unload everyone.
2. Use the **Rotation Tool** on the vehicle and choose **Appearance**.
3. Select Original, Industrial chassis, Classic chassis, Antarctica chassis or
   Hotdog chassis. Wait for **Appearance saved** and the refreshed selection.

The choice applies to that car. Industrial replaces its underbody; the three
Classic choices replace its underbody and wheels. Its original body, family,
carriers, seats, restraint controls and motion stay the same. PAX (including the
legacy `foundation_v1` vehicle) and Pipeline retain their purpose-built Original
running gear. Existing route/operator restrictions
still apply. A moving, occupied, unavailable or stale selection is rejected;
review the refreshed state and correct the reported condition before trying again.

The provider’s full-body styles can add their own seats, colliders and mechanisms.
Those are not replacements for a Fourth Dimension vehicle. Use its normal
Screwdriver on ordinary native carts; use the Rotation Tool’s Appearance screen
on 4D cars. Native skin copying through linking cannot overwrite a 4D car’s
seat or physics state.

The choice is saved with the vehicle. If the provider is absent or its selected
resources are unavailable, the car displays Original and retains its saved
choice. Restoring supported resources makes that choice available again. You
can always deliberately return to Original while the train is empty and ready.
Do not remove an installed mod while Minecraft or its server is running; stop
and save first.
