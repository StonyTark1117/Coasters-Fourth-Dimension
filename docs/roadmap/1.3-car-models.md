# 1.3 — Base-model repair, refinement and body choices

[Guide index and execution rules](README.md) · [Canonical roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md#version-13-car-models) · [Active assignments](../CURRENT-QUALIFICATION.md#current-work-queue)

This release improves existing cars. FD917's accepted production baseline and the
original vehicle behavior remain inputs. Model choices use the 1.1 appearance
path; a new pose, track type, seat layout or propulsion contract needs a
separate vehicle package. The order is defect repair (FD2101), reference review
and base-model refinement (FD2104), then variants (FD2102) and reconciliation
(FD2103). Review and repair can share evidence; variants wait for the accepted
base-model baseline. These cards are procedures; the current queue owns assignments.

## FD2101

**Outcome:** The finite list of evidenced base-model defects is repaired, including
literal gaps, detached supports, wrong moving attachments, clipping and broken
model/texture presentation. This does not alone establish broader model readiness.

**Start conditions:** [FD917](pre-1.0-visual-production.md#fd917) and the affected
vehicle specifications.

**Read first:** [canonical criterion](../../ROADMAP.md#fd2101),
[FD917 production target](pre-1.0-visual-production.md#1-the-production-target),
retained [FD917 art evidence](../evidence/fd917/README.md), and the affected
model source/assets.

**Ordered steps:**

1. Inventory the specific original car assemblies where a seat, bar or joint
   looks detached. Record the affected vehicle, current transform and intended
   physical path from chassis to seat/restraint; name the observed defect. Keep
   confirmed faults separate from refinement candidates and unresolved questions.
   Retain accepted Fixed Inverted and Arrow repair evidence, including the
   distinct axle-to-seat-shell path. A linkage reaching the axle does not by
   itself prove that the seat shell connects to it.
2. Correct the meshes and transforms together so supports follow actual pivots
   and restraints. Keep accepted capacities, seating positions and track fit.
3. Check empty and occupied cars at rest and in the affected motion extrema;
   inspect rider view, restraint state, clipping and sweep where the edit reaches.
4. Update affected item/preview representations if they show the corrected part.

**Validation:** Compare affected views and fit with the named defect. Use the
smallest check that establishes the changed behavior; do not rerun all FD917
families for a local asset correction.

**Finish and handoff:** Record changed models, affected evidence and any
remaining precise defect. Keep each accepted repair closed; the package stays
open while listed defects remain. Feed [FD2104](#fd2104) base readiness and
[FD2103](#fd2103).

## FD2104

**Review record:** [17-style first-pass model/reference matrix and candidate recommendations](../evidence/1.3/model-reference-review.md), plus the [read-only structural follow-up](../evidence/1.3/structural-reference-follow-up.md). The reports are complete; implementation reconciliation remains open, implementation remains planned, and variants still follow FD2101 defect closure.

**Outcome:** Existing base models receive the agreed improvements needed before
selectable variants are derived from them. The review looks beyond FD2101's
direct findings; a connected model can still need substantial visual refinement.

**Start conditions:** The existing 17-style roster, adopted vehicle dimensions,
model generators/assets and retained FD917/FD2101 views. Review can proceed
while defects are repaired; final base acceptance consumes the affected fixes.

**Read first:** [canonical criterion](../../ROADMAP.md#fd2104), family
specifications, retained occupied/exterior/item views and primary real-world
references. Use documented other-game counterparts where they illuminate a
style or presentation technique; distinguish their artistic choices from real
construction and the project's own mechanics.

**Ordered steps:**

1. Compare the complete existing roster at the source/reference-review level.
   For each style record current asset/view paths, reference links, confidence,
   and an explicit disposition: defect to FD2101, refinement candidate, retained
   baseline, or unresolved evidence question. Missing/obscured views do not
   establish that a joint is sound. Do not invent defects from stylistic taste.
2. Assess proportions and recognizable silhouette; chassis/bogie and seat-support
   hierarchy; credible load paths and moving joints; seat, rider, head/leg and
   restraint fit; state readability; textures, materials, detail scale and UV
   quality; and item/preview consistency with the world model. Compare each style
   to its relevant counterpart, not one generic car template.
3. Convert findings into a finite prioritized refinement list with named models,
   concrete before/after targets, source/output ownership and affected validation.
   Include improvements beyond literal gap repairs. Record why retained models
   need no change; distinguish required baseline work from optional future polish.
   Freeze this list before implementation, revising explicitly for new findings.
4. When assigned, implement that agreed base-model work with original assets.
   Keep pivots, seats, restraints, capacity, track and camera contracts explicit.
   Any change touching an accepted rider or swept envelope needs its own affected
   fit check; a new vehicle mechanic needs a separate scope decision.
5. Reconcile completed improvements and defects, update item/preview assets and
   freeze the resulting base geometry/attachment points. Wing and Fixed Inverted
   variant designs consume these refined bases; do not treat their new variants
   as substitutes for unfinished base-model work.

**Validation:** Source/geometry and retained-view review first. Check changed
models in the relevant empty/occupied and restraint/motion states only where
existing evidence cannot establish their new fit. Texture-only edits need no
mechanics replay. Reference review across the roster does not require another
17-style runtime campaign, nor repeated polish after the agreed targets pass.

**Finish and handoff:** Publish the per-style review, finite completed refinement
list, accepted base assets and dimensions, remaining optional polish and affected
evidence. Feed [FD2102](#fd2102) and [FD2103](#fd2103). Broader implementation
is planned until assigned; the current reference-review assignment is read-only.

## FD2102

**Outcome:** Two original geometric body choices, a compact Wing shell and an
open-frame Fixed Inverted shell, are usable and persistent.

**Start conditions:** [FD1603](1.1-tools.md#fd1603) appearance selection and
accepted [FD2101](#fd2101) defect repairs and [FD2104](#fd2104) base-model
refinement, with frozen Wing/Fixed Inverted dimensions. Do not begin variant
implementation before the agreed base-model work is complete.

**Read first:** [canonical criterion](../../ROADMAP.md#fd2102),
[builder tool contract](../BUILDER-TOOLS.md),
[Fixed Inverted specification](../FIXED-INVERTED.md),
[Wing specification](../WING.md),
and the affected production assets.

**Ordered steps:**

1. Freeze a small roster with one named body choice per target vehicle and
   record its visual differences, attachment points, clearance envelope and
   unchanged base vehicle contract.
2. Build the Wing and Fixed Inverted geometry and any attached
   restraint/support parts; preserve each vehicle's seat pivots, camera,
   motion and state cues.
3. Add both choices to the existing model/appearance selection and preview.
   Preserve the chosen identifier on save/load; use the base model if a choice
   is unavailable without changing vehicle identity or rider state.
4. Check normal construction, boarding, riding, stopping and reload with each
   choice. Compare the occupied outer sweep, restraint pose, item and preview
   against its base vehicle.

**Validation:** Inspect both variants in player views and use targeted
persistence/fallback and fit checks. Retain accepted Wing/Fixed Inverted behavior
evidence where no contract changed.

**Finish and handoff:** Record the supported roster and affected evidence;
feed [FD2103](#fd2103). Thrill Glider-inspired suspended flying-bike behavior
belongs to [FD2204](1.4-specialty-vehicles.md#fd2204).

## FD2103

**Outcome:** The finite 1.3 car-model release is reconciled.

**Start conditions:** [FD2101](#fd2101), [FD2104](#fd2104) and [FD2102](#fd2102).

**Read first:** [canonical criterion](../../ROADMAP.md#fd2103),
[validation policy](../VALIDATION-POLICY.md), and current affected evidence.

**Ordered steps:**

1. Reconcile the finite defect and refinement lists, per-style review dispositions,
   accepted base geometry, corrected supports, both distinct body choices, occupied fit,
   appearance selection, persistence and fallback against their package claims.
2. Name any unmet required criterion and route it to its owner; retain accepted
   FD917, Wing, Flying and 1.1 tool evidence unless a named change invalidates a
   specific claim.
3. Freeze the model roster and update the canonical status/current queue when
   assigned work is complete. Reconcile only affected release content/checks.

**Validation:** Evidence review unless a named affected claim remains unproven.

**Finish and handoff:** Close the 1.3 gate from its three package outcomes;
publication remains separate.
