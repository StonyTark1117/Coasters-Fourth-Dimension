# 1.4 — Specialty vehicles

[Guide index and execution rules](README.md) · [Canonical roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md#version-14-specialty-vehicles) · [Active assignments](../CURRENT-QUALIFICATION.md#current-work-queue)

Two finite new styles are in scope: one fixed-standing coaster and one
straddle-seated motorbike coaster. Each owns its complete specification,
mechanics, original content and player acceptance. They can proceed
independently after their shared prerequisites; neither waits for 1.3 model
choices or the post-1.0 moving-track program. These cards are procedures, not
active assignments.

## FD2201

**Outcome:** One original, fully usable standing coaster vehicle.

**Start conditions:** [FD1101A](0.5-contracts.md#fd1101a),
[FD403](0.4-core.md#fd403), [FD917](pre-1.0-visual-production.md#fd917).

**Read first:** [canonical criterion](../../ROADMAP.md#fd2201),
[vehicle acceptance contract](../../ROADMAP.md#additional-vehicle-and-supporting-feature-packages),
[B&M Surf reference](https://www.bolliger-mabillard.com/surf-coaster),
and accepted shared seat/camera and recovery evidence.

**Ordered steps:**

1. Specify one finite train/seat configuration: fixed standing pose, platform
   and entry geometry, support/restraint attachment, track eligibility,
   measured occupied sweep and safe unload location. Record what the reference
   inspires without assuming a Surf spring mechanism.
2. Implement the new vehicle identity and its standing rider pose, restraint
   interlocks, dispatch guard, camera ownership and stop/recovery path. Prove
   the affected occupied handoff early and reuse it for acceptance.
3. Deliver original car/equipment assets, recipe, controls/guide and playable
   example showing boarding, dispatch and unload.
4. Check normal player construction and riding, occupied fit at the measured
   limits, restraint denial, stop/evacuation, interruption and restart. Use
   targeted checks for any shared contract changed by this vehicle.

**Validation:** Combine native invariants with real occupied player interaction
and affected rendering/fit; reuse shared-contract proof where it still applies.

**Finish and handoff:** Record the frozen configuration, integrated contract,
content and accepted evidence for [FD2203](#fd2203).

## FD2202

**Outcome:** One original, fully usable straddle-seated motorbike vehicle.

**Start conditions:** [FD1101A](0.5-contracts.md#fd1101a),
[FD403](0.4-core.md#fd403), [FD917](pre-1.0-visual-production.md#fd917).

**Read first:** [canonical criterion](../../ROADMAP.md#fd2202),
[vehicle acceptance contract](../../ROADMAP.md#additional-vehicle-and-supporting-feature-packages),
[Vekoma Motorbike reference](https://www.vekoma.com/motorbike-coasters),
and accepted shared seat/camera and recovery evidence.

**Ordered steps:**

1. Specify one finite train/seat configuration: straddle pose, grip/support
   locations, hold-down restraint, platform access, track eligibility,
   measured occupied sweep and dismount location.
2. Implement the new vehicle identity, rider pose/camera, restraint and
   dispatch guards, stop/recovery path and safe reload behavior. Prove the
   affected occupied handoff early and reuse it for acceptance.
3. Deliver original car/equipment assets, recipe, controls/guide and playable
   example showing boarding, dispatch and unload.
4. Check normal player construction and riding, occupied fit at measured
   limits, denied dispatch with an open restraint, stop/evacuation,
   interruption and restart. Use targeted checks for changed shared contracts.

**Validation:** Combine native invariants with real occupied player interaction
and affected rendering/fit; retain unchanged 1.0 vehicle evidence.

**Finish and handoff:** Record the frozen configuration, integrated contract,
content and accepted evidence for [FD2203](#fd2203).

## FD2203

**Outcome:** The finite 1.4 specialty-vehicle release is reconciled.

**Start conditions:** [FD2201](#fd2201) and [FD2202](#fd2202).

**Read first:** [canonical criterion](../../ROADMAP.md#fd2203),
[validation policy](../VALIDATION-POLICY.md), and current package evidence.

**Ordered steps:**

1. Reconcile each vehicle's specified configuration, construction, riding,
   restraint, recovery, original content and affected integration checks.
2. Freeze the supported style/profile list. Name only unmet required criteria
   and route them to their owning package; do not replay unrelated 1.0 work.
3. Update the canonical status/current queue after the two outcomes are
   complete and reconcile affected release documentation and packaging.

**Validation:** Evidence review unless a named required claim remains unproven
or a changed shared contract requires a targeted check.

**Finish and handoff:** Close 1.4 from its two specialty outcomes;
publication remains separate.
