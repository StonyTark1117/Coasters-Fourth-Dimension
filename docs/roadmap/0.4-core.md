# 0.4 — Finish the existing core

[Guide index and execution rules](README.md) · [Canonical roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) · [Active assignments](../CURRENT-QUALIFICATION.md#current-work-queue)

Start with the assigned package's current queue and canonical status. Closed
recovery and teaching cases are inputs; a retained procedure is not an assignment
or a replay request.

Card order helps navigation; actual prerequisites determine readiness. These are
procedures, not a second status tracker. Apply the index's evidence and stopping
rules to every step; already-covered steps do not require another run.

## Choose the assigned package

**An FD402 assignment ends at FD402.** It does not authorize taking on or
launching an agent/chat for FD403. Technical readiness and task authorization
are separate: FD403 can proceed independently when assigned, but it is not a
fallback task for an FD402 owner whose test is blocked or already owned.

| Assigned outcome | Owns | Completion boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FD402 — recovery towing** | Attaching the certified ordinary bearing, towing retained vehicles, braking/detaching and interruptions of that recovery state. | Close its A–F criteria from cumulative evidence. FD403 and FD404 may remain open. Start at the [recovery queue](../CURRENT-QUALIFICATION.md#fd402-recovery-cases). |
| **FD403 — building and teaching** | Normal player crafting/placement, usable powered rides, family controls, capture/unload, save/reopen and walkthroughs. | Close its family/teaching criteria from cumulative evidence. FD402 may remain open. Start at the [construction and teaching queue](../CURRENT-QUALIFICATION.md#fd403-construction-and-teaching-cases). |
| **FD404 — 0.4 checkpoint** | Reconcile completed FD401, FD402 and FD403 results. | This parent requires both siblings; neither sibling requires the other. No fresh sweep. |

Route a claim by **what the vehicle is doing**, not a shared word or API:

- Station capture/unload during a normal ride and a normal PAX winch cycle are
  FD403 walkthrough behavior. Attaching a bare bearing, Start tow and a recovery
  link surviving disconnection/removal are FD402 behavior. A normal ride's fault
  recovery is not automatically ordinary-bearing towing.
- FD402 can reuse retained fixtures and accepted operator inputs. It does not
  require rebuilding its recovery route through survival crafting. FD403's
  player-built-route claim still needs actual player input where unproven; a
  command-built recovery fixture does not establish that claim.
- Normal ride save/reopen and braked attached-recovery restart are different
  claims. Share unchanged identity/persistence evidence where relevant, but
  check the unproven recovery-link interaction only in its recovery case.
- A defect in a genuinely shared path gets one implementation owner and the
  named affected checks. Cite that fix in both packages if needed; do not import
  the other package's entire checklist or reset its accepted siblings.

An owner waiting on another recovery run can prepare its own unresolved case,
diagnose its fixture or reconcile relevant evidence within FD402. If none can
advance, record the exact blocker and needed handoff. Do not substitute an
unassigned package, create another chat or claim a duplicate runtime to stay busy.
Reading the sibling boundary here is enough; closing FD402 does not require
reading or executing the FD403 procedure.

## FD402

**Outcome:** Evaluate and document recovery towing reconciled from its named deliverables.

**Start conditions:** FD108, FD106 and FD305; confirm the current recovery case owner. Read accepted child results as inputs. FD403 is not a prerequisite or part of this assignment.

**Read first:** [canonical scope, status and completion criteria](../../ROADMAP.md#fd402-evaluate-and-document-recovery-towing); [canonical status and evidence](../CURRENT-QUALIFICATION.md), [gate blockers and follow-up work](../VALIDATION-POLICY.md#gate-blockers-and-follow-up-work).

**Ordered steps:**

1. Read the canonical A–F statuses and the recovery-only queue. Extract only open FD402 cases; already accepted mechanics, visuals, operator inputs, persistence and optional-power results do not become new work because their cards remain below.
2. Complete the assigned unresolved recovery case using its retained setup and evidence, or name its exact blocker and needed ownership handoff. Another agent owning a case does not make FD403 part of the assignment.
3. When the children are covered, reconcile their evidence against the existing recovery envelope and make RECOVERY.md match it. Extra assemblies, routes, survival rebuilding and drive settings are not implicit remaining work.
4. Close FD402 and its active case entries when its A–F criteria pass. Hand the result to FD404 and FD902B; do not wait for FD403 or replay accepted child workflows.

**Validation:** Evidence review only unless a named required claim is still missing or affected. An aggregate label does not require a fresh run.

**Finish and handoff:** Close recovery scope for [FD404](0.4-core.md#fd404); [FD902B](1.0-release.md#fd902b) consumes the same lifecycle records. Record evidence and closure in the canonical package/current queue as applicable, then stop.
