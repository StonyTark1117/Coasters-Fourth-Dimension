## FD1701B

**Outcome:** A usable ride-local multi-train system and example on one declared circuit.

**Start conditions:** [FD1701A](0.8-operations.md#fd1701a)

**Read first:** [canonical scope, status and completion criteria](../../ROADMAP.md#fd1701b); [ProfileEditor](../../src/main/java/dev/fourthdimension/track/ProfileEditor.java), [AppearanceEditor](../../src/main/java/dev/fourthdimension/integration/AppearanceEditor.java), [RideTelemetry](../../src/main/java/dev/fourthdimension/integration/RideTelemetry.java), [RideOperations](../../src/main/java/dev/fourthdimension/integration/RideOperations.java), [tool scope](../../PLAN.md#d19-vehicle-variants-tools-and-scenery-facing-yaw).

**Ordered steps:**

1. Consume [FD1701A](0.8-operations.md#fd1701a) and inspect integrated upstream block capabilities; specify the fixed circuit, finite train count and supported homogeneous consists.
2. Implement whole-consist block occupancy, route reservations and train admission under the selected owners.
3. Enforce station dispatch/braking separation; unresolved occupancy cannot grant another train entry.
4. Preserve vehicle identities, occupancy and reservations across train removal, chunk unload, interruption and restart; implement the declared recovery path.
5. Deliver normal controls, diagnostics, guide and a multi-train example at the specified limit; exclude arbitrary junction networks, automatic depots and mixed-family consists.

**Validation:** Native occupancy/reservation/authority invariants and functional player operation; retain [FD1701A](0.8-operations.md#fd1701a)'s shared authority proof.

**Finish and handoff:** Hand implementation and example evidence to [FD1701C](1.2-multi-train.md#fd1701c) without replaying Dive or Powered Inverted. Record evidence and closure in the canonical package/current queue as applicable, then stop.

## FD1701C

**Outcome:** Accepted separation, dispatch and interruption recovery at the declared train limit.

**Start conditions:** [FD1701B](1.2-multi-train.md#fd1701b)

**Read first:** [canonical scope, status and completion criteria](../../ROADMAP.md#fd1701c); [cumulative acceptance](../VALIDATION-POLICY.md), [shared occupied proof](../../ROADMAP.md#shared-contract-handoff), [current evidence](../CURRENT-QUALIFICATION.md).

**Ordered steps:**

1. Reconcile [FD1701B](1.2-multi-train.md#fd1701b)'s native reservation and example evidence against its fixed-route/train-count claims.
2. Complete uncovered normal occupied admission, station dispatch and separation observations using the declared consists.
3. Check distinct removal, unload, interruption and restart cases with unresolved occupancy denied entry; reuse matching authority evidence.
4. Record cumulative coverage and close the bounded operations outcome without a park-wide matrix.

**Validation:** Native reservation/state assertions combined with occupied interaction for missing player/synchronization claims.

**Finish and handoff:** Close [FD1701C](1.2-multi-train.md#fd1701c), then [FD1701](1.2-multi-train.md#fd1701) and [FD1703](1.2-multi-train.md#fd1703) from the same evidence. Record evidence and closure in the canonical package/current queue as applicable, then stop.
