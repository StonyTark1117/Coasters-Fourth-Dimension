procedures, not a second status tracker. Apply the index's evidence and stopping

## FD1501A

**Outcome:** Working S1 conventional Spinning mechanics and its required shared-contract handoff.

**Start conditions:** [FD501A](0.5-contracts.md#fd501a), [FD1101A](0.5-contracts.md#fd1101a), [FD1501](0.5-contracts.md#fd1501)

**Read first:** [canonical scope, status and completion criteria](../../ROADMAP.md#fd1501a); [VehicleContract](../../src/main/java/dev/fourthdimension/contract/VehicleContract.java), [VehicleFactory](../../src/main/java/dev/fourthdimension/vehicle/VehicleFactory.java), [CarrierRuntime](../../src/main/java/dev/fourthdimension/runtime/CarrierRuntime.java), [occupied handoff](../../ROADMAP.md#shared-contract-handoff).

**Ordered steps:**

1. Consume [FD1501](0.5-contracts.md#fd1501) and the integrated prerequisites; identify reusable mechanics, lifecycle and occupied evidence before changing shared code.
2. Implement the specified local-upright carrier axis/pivot through S1; preserve original carrier-axis behavior in shared paths.
3. Apply load-sensitive free dynamics, damping and the declared speed/range limits with the specified seating and track fit.
4. Implement guarded station alignment/capture, restraint release and the specified rollback/power-loss handling.
5. Check affected native dynamics, eligibility, clearance and failure behavior, concentrating on asymmetric loading, new-axis clearance and capture interruption.
6. Complete the representative connected-client occupied boarding → motion → capture/stop → unload handoff with functional geometry. Check a new/changed synchronization path with a second real client; otherwise reuse accepted remote-rider coverage.
7. Integrate the passing contract into main and record its inputs, evidence and consumer handoff; do not wait for decorative polish.

**Validation:** Native checks cover the affected mechanism/invariants; connected clients cover normal input and rider/body/camera ownership. Retain unchanged prerequisite evidence. Asymmetric loading, new-axis clearance and capture interruption are the feature-specific focus.

**Finish and handoff:** Close [FD1501A](0.6-motion.md#fd1501a) under its canonical criteria; hand the integrated interface to [FD1301A](0.6-motion.md#fd1301a), [FD1302A](0.7-complex-motion.md#fd1302a) and [FD1501D](0.8-operations.md#fd1501d). [FD1501C](0.6-motion.md#fd1501c) reuses the occupied proof. Record evidence and closure in the canonical package/current queue as applicable, then stop.
