## Walkthrough points

The connected records cover the complete player-facing control sequence:

1. Discover and craft Legacy ZacSpin Track and the matching Legacy ZacSpin Car
   from the ordinary result slots.
2. Connect matching anchors, place the car, board an original seat with empty
   hands, and close restraints with the Rotation Tool.
3. Keep the Legacy distinction explicit: its two back-to-back carriers are
   free carriers driven by gravity, track motion and rider loading. Section
   angle keys do not drive them.
4. Tune damping and ballast on the car, and expect the two carrier groups to
   respond differently when rider loading differs.
5. Request capture and wait for both carriers to align and stop before opening
   restraints and dismounting.

The flat survival route records crafting, placement, occupied powered travel,
capture, unload and cold reopen. The shaped demonstration records the distinct
free-response observation: both carriers released on a shaped powered circuit,
developed different angles and angular speeds, and later returned to capture
before normal unload and reopen. Together these observations support the
walkthrough wording and its controls. The shaped demonstration was
command-authored, so it remains supporting teaching/mechanics evidence only.
