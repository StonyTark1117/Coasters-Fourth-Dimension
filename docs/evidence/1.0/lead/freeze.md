## Frozen capabilities and limits

All17 styles are included: Arrow, Legacy ZacSpin, Free Spin, Revised ZacSpin,
PAX, Wing, S1 Spinning, S2 Spinning, Suspended, Fixed Inverted, Axis, Roller Ball,
Conventional Flying, New-generation Flying, Pipeline, Dive and Powered Inverted
Yaw. Each retains its accepted capacity, seats, native body/constraint, track,
station/restraint, normal operation and interruption/recovery contract. Required
recipes, examples, guides and teaching content feed the accepted family outcomes.

Included shared behavior comprises strict family/track eligibility and provenance,
linked-consist guards, saved profiles/banks, magnetic spans, ordinary station and
restraint controls, declared FE/native drives, recovery, ride-local authority,
remote rider/pose synchronization and selectable comfort/effects composition.
Optional integrations are the specifically accepted Styles cosmetic ownership,
Engineered drive/control, Additions station/seat, measured CTNB functional rails,
Magnetized drive, Skin appearance, Immersion client effects, Waterparked slide
separation and Create Coasters native utilities. Their presence never grants
unsupported track eligibility or a second seat/drive authority.

The [canonical envelope](../../../../ROADMAP.md#supported-envelope-and-explicit-10-dispositions)
remains ±1024 x/z; dimension admission limits256 sections,64 vehicles,8192 profile
keys,512 magnetic spans and at most8 spans/section. These are correctness/storage
limits, not demonstrated park capacity. Recovery remains restricted to FD402's
accepted bearing/route/family/drive/equipment/provider set. Unknown schema,
foreign/missing geometry and unsupported conversions fail closed. FD801's external
functional Arrow provider, standalone builder tools, full multi-train management,
and moving track remain outside this freeze. No new livery or family is added.
