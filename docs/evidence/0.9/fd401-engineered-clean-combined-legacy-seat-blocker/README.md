On 2026-11-05, a Java 21 packaged client loaded the committed-source `ec2fcda` qualification JAR (SHA-256 `656725bcbacc8f491de274c6c058027d0aaa67cfc6f893ba6d7e4a9eb24abbc7`) with Track Styles 1.2.0 and Engineered 1.1.1. Crafts & Additions was a disclosed FE fixture dependency. The [dependency hashes](dependencies.sha256) were checked immediately before the run. The [launch script](run-client.sh) used the isolated committed-source export/build at `/tmp/fd401-a-source-ec2fcda` and `/tmp/fd401-a-ec2fcda-build`, whose packaged JAR matched the selected hash. The copied [world](final-world.tar.gz) began from the stopped four-family construction world in the [earlier construction record](../fd401-engineered-current-combined-construction/README.md), not a newly player-built route.

Both attempts used task-owned private Xvfb `:240`, a direct Java `-Xmx1024M` game heap and an active `systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=3G` client scope. The successful second launch's scope was `run-p304997-i324743.scope`; while active, `MemoryMax=3221225472` and `MemoryCurrent=2473037824` were observed. It exited with `Result=success` after Save and Quit to Title, then Quit Game. The owned display was stopped after the client exited. The first attempt also exited normally. The host had 33 GiB available and the user-service cgroup had no hard limit before the second launch.

The first attempt used `/fd validation board_locked Dev` after teleporting near the fresh Legacy route. That helper searches all ready vehicles in saved order and **boarded the old Arrow car** at about `(100.5,146.4,132.5)`, so it gives no Legacy acceptance. The first [console](first-attempt-console.log.gz) is retained only to disclose that error. No Legacy cycle was attempted in that world.

The second attempt restored a new copy of the untouched construction world. The player set gravity to zero as a camera fixture, teleported to the Legacy route near `(300,183,120)`, and initialized `CoastersEngineeredEnergy=100000` on its anchor with a disclosed `/data merge block` command. A subsequent `/data get block` returned `100000` ([screenshot](legacy-fe.png)). The player approached the normally placed Legacy car at `(300.5,185.3,126.5)` and attempted empty-hand right clicks on its visible seat. The [F3 screenshot](target-f3.png) shows the crosshair targeting `fourthdimension:legacy_seat`, rather than a preview or a different family block. The offline saved Legacy record has restraints open, `boarding_ready=1`, both carrier captures true, and near-zero carrier angle/omega. The live client still did not mount. `/data get entity Dev RootVehicle` returned no element and `/fd validation availability` returned `assignments=0` ([screenshot](legacy-no-mount.png)); the [game log](game.log.gz) has no `FD_BOARDING_EVENT` for this attempt. The exact UI inputs and fixture commands are retained in [commands.txt](commands.txt) and the full [console](console.log.gz).

This is **not an FD401C operator pass**. The observed Legacy seat interaction failed in this copied fixture, and the run stopped before launch/STOP/capture/unload. The record does not prove whether the cause is the saved fixture's runtime readiness, reach/interception of the seat interaction, or a production bug. A targeted server-side diagnosis of `Boarding.canBoard/ready` and the live seat interaction is needed before another combined-client operator attempt. The world and logs are retained to avoid repeating the setup or aim loop without new information.
