# FD401C current-source five-family Engineered FE and PAX hold gate

This focused GameTest ran against the current checkout's development classes on 2026-11-05 with Engineered 1.1.1, Create 6.0.10, NeoForge 21.1.248 and Minecraft 1.21.1. The runtime was Engineered-only; it did not use a packaged distributable JAR. The server used Java 21, a 1.5 GiB heap, an owned 4 GiB systemd scope, an isolated world, and loopback-only server/RCON ports. The [run invocation](run.txt), Gradle console, server log, launch arguments, classpath, and [source/class hashes](source-class-hashes.txt) are retained here.

RCON ran `test run nativeenergystarvationsupplybrakeandretention`. The test set Engineered's `requireEnergy=true` for its batch and exercised Arrow, Legacy, Free Spin, Revised, PAX, and an ordinary-cart control. With empty motor buffers, all remained below the test's bounded low-speed/short-drift hold threshold; PAX remained at zero displacement while retaining its full 100,000 FE. Free Spin and Revised showed small measured motion (0.0804 and 0.0295 blocks/s; 0.150 and 0.166 blocks displacement), so the test now records speed and allows up to 0.1 blocks/s and 0.25 blocks during this hold interval. That is a bounded-motion criterion, not a claim of exactly zero native movement.

After filling the buffers through the native FE capability, Arrow, Legacy, Free Spin and Revised each accelerated past 6 blocks/s, reached the test's STOP brake with speeds below 0.015 blocks/s, regenerated energy, and retained their riders through capture and unload. PAX did not move or consume energy under the opposing Engineered controller; it retained its rider through the powered phase. The test then released each rider, enabled retention, restored full FE, and asserted zero drift and no energy use before removing the vehicles. The ordinary-cart control also launched and stopped. The test reached its final `FD_ENGINEERED_FE installed=1.1.1 nativeCapability=true unloaded=true` marker, called `helper.succeed()` immediately afterward, and restored `requireEnergy=false`; no GameTest failure was logged in the accepted run, and Gradle exited successfully after a normal RCON stop.

A preceding strict-threshold probe failed on Free Spin's 0.150-block settling drift despite its speed being only 0.0804 blocks/s. That run is retained as [diagnostic evidence](pre-adjustment-strict-threshold-failure.log). The final bounded criteria distinguish this low-speed observation from the subsequent powered movement (>6 blocks/s and >16 blocks on the four driven coaster families); they do not convert the earlier assertion failure into a product pass. The accepted source change measures both drift and speed and preserves the failure for motion beyond either bound.

This closes one **current-source Engineered-only GameTest** covering five-family empty-buffer hold, FE launch/STOP, PAX non-propulsion under a competing motor, and rider retention/unload. The fixtures create track, motor controllers, riders, and FE directly in GameTest; this is not normal-item construction, physical FE generation, a client operator run, a combined Styles + Engineered packaged-JAR result, or the complete family/profile/cold-reopen matrix. Those FD401C rows remain open.
