# FD401C selected-JAR starvation, recharge hold, and full circuit

This run closes the previously open **full geographic circuit after FE starvation and source restoration** slice on the exact selected JAR. It used `coasters-fourth-dimension-0.9.0-dev.27.jar`, SHA-256 `656725bcbacc8f491de274c6c058027d0aaa67cfc6f893ba6d7e4a9eb24abbc7`, with Track Styles 1.2.0 and Engineered 1.1.1 loaded together and Engineered `requireEnergy=true`. Java 21.0.13 ran the client on private Xvfb `:343`; the game heap was 1536M and the active systemd scope was capped at 3 GiB. The scope finished with `Result=success`.

The client used a fresh copy of the preserved [input checkpoint](input-world.tar.gz). Both original north-facing Create water wheels at `(-507,142,-635)` and `(-507,142,-636)` were removed and the anchor buffer at `(-500,142,-630)` was cleared. After a normal empty-hand seat click, the log recorded `FD_BOARDING_ASSIGN ... accepted=true`; Dev closed restraints in the Rotation Tool UI. Releasing station redstone and pulsing the linked controller at `(-506,142,-628)` produced `CoastersEngineeredStarving=1b` while `assignments=1` and the rider remained aboard. The anchor had no `CoastersEngineeredEnergy` tag and the alternator reported speed 0.

While the car remained at the station, the station hold was re-engaged and both wheels were restored at their original positions and orientation. The alternator returned to 8 RPM with capacity 768 and stress 512. The wired anchor charged to 25,102 FE before dispatch. The recovered run recorded `CoastersEngineeredPassStarved=0b`; the occupied car moved through `(-493.9,143.1,-637.2)` and returned to `(-499.5,143.1,-617.5)`. It was initially uncaptured at 0.0159 blocks/s. Restoring station redstone brought validation to `capture=true` at the same station position. The recorded pass reached `19.4068698883/20` and retained `PassShort=1`: this proves the post-starvation geographic circuit and station capture, but does **not** clear the controller's 20/20 speed gate.

Dev opened restraints through the normal Rotation Tool UI and used Left Shift to dismount. The server logged `FD_BOARDING_DISMOUNT_CLEAR player=Dev restraints=false removed=false alive=true`; `RootVehicle` had no matching element and validation reported `chassis=1/1 carriers=2/2 assignments=0`. The final saved [world](final-world.tar.gz) retained the powered 8 RPM source, station capture, and 53,794 FE in the anchor. The client saved, returned to title, and exited normally.
