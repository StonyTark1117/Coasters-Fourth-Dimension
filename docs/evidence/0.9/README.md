# Evidence toward the 0.9 candidate

[Recovery schema migration, dev.27](recovery-schema-migration.md) verifies the
supported schema-1–5 loader contract for occupied five-family recovery records.
It also pins the recorded 0.3 schema-3 world extract as a current-loader,
schema-6-reopen regression. A separate [complete schema-3 world replay](native-schema3-world-replay.md)
upgrades a real historical packaged-server
save with its native Sable sublevels and reopens all 3 chassis and 8 carriers.
A [connected-client Arrow ride](native-schema3-client-ride.md) then boards,
dispatches, captures, and dismounts in that upgraded world. Separate bounded
[Legacy and Free Spin operator cycles](native-schema3-legacy-free-spin-operator.md)
now prove client seat/editor/restraint/unload paths on those migrated cars;
sustained historical rides, provider conversion and broader historical-world
acceptance remain open.

[Appearance provider removal](appearance-provider-lifecycle.md) combines a
recorded save/remove/reinstall cycle with a current-schema absent-provider
GameTest. It verifies cosmetic-provider loss does not alter a functional rail,
but does not establish external functional-track conversion.

[PAX shuttle support-recovery checkpoint](pax-shuttle-support-recovery.md)
records an occupied active-lift support loss, exact repair, explicit physical
realignment, guarded winch recovery, and powered relaunch of the original
four-car train.

[Engineered connected-client operator check](fd401-engineered-client-operator.md)
records native motor-span placement, linked controller configuration, a bounded
occupied ride consuming 2,999 FE, client-operated capture and unloading, and
cold persistence with the pinned Engineered addon. The short fixture ended at
the track-boundary safety stop; that check did not cover a complete circuit or
an external FE network.

[Engineered real FE-network transfer](fd401-engineered-fe-network.md) adds a
validation-only Crafts & Additions Creative Generator and client-linked wire.
The empty motor anchor fills to 100,000 FE through that network and refills
after a server cold restart. That transfer check did not include a ride, and
this extra mod is not an advertised compatibility profile.

[Engineered network-powered Arrow loop](fd401-engineered-network-loop.md)
records a connected-client occupied return around a complete original circuit
without native chain drives. The linked generator network powered the motor;
the client used the Rotation Tool editor to dispatch, capture and unload. The
car was placed with a validation fixture after ordinary item clicks failed,
and redstone station operation remains untested.

[World upgrade and recovery procedure](../../MIGRATION.md) gives the supported
backup, staged-upgrade and restore steps. It does not convert third-party

[Five-family support loss, dev.27](five-family-support-loss.md) verifies that
an occupied native car fails closed when its only matching track connection is
removed. It preserves rider ownership, matching binding, carrier capture, and
closed restraints across all five families, then permanently removes one
carrier from every family and verifies rider recovery with no retained native
or saved vehicle state. Live late-join is covered
separately; occupied clients, disconnect and full lifecycle coverage remain
FD902 work.

[Five-family physical support recovery](five-family-support-repair.md) now
requires an explicit two-step recovery after support loss: **Repair matching
track** accepts only the saved geometry fingerprint, then **Realign matching
track** physically re-engages the original native bearing before dispatch. The
server checkpoint and [packaged occupied five-family client realignment](client-occupied-five-family-support-realignment.md)
cover all five families with retained riders, moving support loss, exact repair,
and client-side bearing placement. The native test retains two independent
server-player seat assignments per family through support loss, exact repair,
physical realignment, dispatch interlock, and Sable holding-store reload. PAX
covers its real four-chassis train with a full-consist emergency native-velocity
reset before reattachment. [two-client Arrow server recovery](two-client-arrow-support-recovery.md)
and the [five-family server matrix](two-client-five-family-server-recovery.md)
now carry Dev and Observer through moving support loss, exact repair, and
physical realignment. The [ordered two-client five-family recovery](two-client-five-family-ordered-recovery.md) now confirms both replicas observe loss before repair before realignment. PAX lift dispatch and post-repair client reconnect or cold-restart coverage remain FD902 work.

An exact standalone candidate then exposed an occupied Arrow operator blocker at a two-section endpoint: after a normal repair, the boundary guard replaced the cleared support-loss fault while realignment remained required, so the normal realignment control rejected. The failed case is preserved in the [Arrow repair blocker record](create-telemetry/de667f7-arrow-repair-blocker/README.md). The release branch now prevents a pending realignment from being replaced by a route-boundary fault, can recover the historical boundary-fault state, and atomically stops captured native bodies in the explicit realignment action. The exact standalone [native retest](create-telemetry/effa1c6-native-hard-stop-retest/README.md) passes all 47 required GameTests. The packaged [fresh cycle](create-telemetry/effa1c6-realignment-retest/README.md) and [preserved historical replay](create-telemetry/311078e-historical-recovery/README.md) both reach normal reattachment and unloading. The historical endpoint then reports its genuine end-of-compatible-track warning; neither replay claims dispatch through that incomplete route or restores broader end-to-end acceptance.
